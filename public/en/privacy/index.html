DATA AND LOCAL CONNECTION

Privacy policy.

See what information the site receives, what you share with support, and what stays on your computer.

Website visits

The hosting provider may receive your IP address, request time, requested route and browser information to deliver pages, diagnose errors and protect the service.

Time calculator and Stream Deck

The calculator runs in your browser. Stream Deck keeps the PIN in browser memory and sends commands directly to your PC over HTTP on the local network. The site host does not receive your PIN, macro state or documents processed by the app.

Email support

When you email support, you share your address, message and any attachments with support and its email provider. Keep passwords, PINs and other people's personal data out of support messages.

Retention

Hosting logs follow the provider's configuration and retention periods. Support messages may be retained as needed to respond, follow up and meet applicable obligations.

Optional analytics

When configured and authorized by you, Google Analytics measures visits, clicks and counts of technical errors on the Portuguese homepage. You can review your choice in that page's footer. Stream Deck is outside this measurement.
