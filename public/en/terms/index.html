ACCESS TERMS

Terms of use.

These terms cover the Automatizador PRO website, documentation, time calculator and access to the local Stream Deck.

Website and calculator

The website describes the product and how to use it. The calculator uses illustrative estimates. App access depends on authorized distribution. Requesting access by email does not create a purchase or subscription.

Authorized use

Only automate devices, accounts, data and systems you are authorized to use. Follow the terms of the destination services and applicable obligations for the data you process. Unauthorized access to another person's computer is prohibited.

Running macros

Real macros can click, press keys and change data in the active app. Test with sample data, verify the target window and fields, and keep backups when needed. Image detection, OCR and document extraction can require human review.

Local connection

Stream Deck requires the PC, app and local panel to stay on. Use a trusted network, keep your PIN private and turn off the panel when finished. The PC connection uses HTTP on the local network.

Privacy and contact

See our Privacy Policy and data rights page. Contact support with questions. Your rights under applicable law still apply.
