PERSONAL DATA

Your rights under Brazil's LGPD.

Use this channel to request information or exercise rights regarding personal data handled through the site and support.

What you can request

You can request confirmation of processing, access, correction and information on sharing. Depending on the circumstances, you can also request anonymization, blocking, deletion or portability, withdraw consent, or object. See the ANPD guidance on data subject rights.

How to submit a request

Email the right you wish to exercise and the context needed to identify your support request. We may ask for further details to verify your identity when needed. Avoid sending identity documents or sensitive information in your first message.
