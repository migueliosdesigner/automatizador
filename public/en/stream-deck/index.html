PC CONNECTION

Control through your local network.

  1. On Windows, open Settings → Mobile. Enter this website's domain in Website HTTPS domain and start the panel.
  2. Connect your phone and PC to the same network.
  3. Enter the address and PIN shown in the app. Allow local network access if your browser prompts you.
